About Sylvan Lake

Real estate in Sylvan Lake

Sylvan Lake blends resort-town lifestyle with year-round living. From lakeview homes and recreational properties to family neighbourhoods and vacation getaways, it draws buyers from across Alberta. If you're dreaming of life by the water — or selling a Sylvan Lake property — I can help you navigate this unique market.

Sylvan Lake's market has its own rhythm, with seasonal swings and premium lakeview pricing. Local knowledge makes the difference between overpaying and getting real value.

How I help Sylvan Lake buyers & sellers

  • Lakeview homes, cabins and recreational properties
  • Year-round family homes near schools and amenities
  • Vacation and second-home purchases
  • Selling strategies tailored to Sylvan Lake's seasonal demand
