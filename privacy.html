Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Missy Foley ("I", "me", "my"), a licensed real estate agent operating in association with Royal LePage, collects, uses and protects the personal information of visitors to this website. I am committed to handling your information responsibly and in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

Information I collect

I only collect personal information that you choose to provide or that is necessary to serve you, which may include:

  • Contact details you submit through the website's contact form, such as your name, email address and phone number;
  • Details about your real estate needs that you share with me (for example, whether you're buying, selling or looking for a market evaluation);
  • Basic technical information your browser sends automatically, such as your device type and general location, which may be collected through standard website analytics.

How I use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide the real estate services you request;
  • Send you property information, market evaluations or listings relevant to your request;
  • Improve this website and understand how visitors use it.

I will not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

Property search & Royal LePage

This website links to a property search hosted by Royal LePage. When you use that search or submit your details there, your information is handled under Royal LePage's own privacy practices. Please review the Royal LePage privacy policy for details on how they manage your data.

Third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as website hosting, email, and analytics providers. These providers may process limited information on my behalf and are expected to protect it appropriately.

Cookies & analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand site usage and improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Data retention & security

I keep personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above or as required by law, and I take reasonable steps to protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorized access.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information I hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to further contact at any time. To make a request, contact me using the details below.
